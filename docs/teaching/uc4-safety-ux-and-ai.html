F1 · fixes the visibility complaint
Subject‑Safety Graph Canvas ⭐

F2 · the headline “AI does the work”
Agentic Remediate agent
One click: explain the finding, draft the concrete change‑set (add allergy record, deconflict the Rx pair, book the follow‑up), preview the diff, route for approval.
Architecture, reuse & invariant ▾
ArchitectureThe model proposes a structured op + diff; a human commits it. Reported to cut investigation cycle time 40–50%.
Reusebff/src/hitl/ops.ts — the only authorized writer (Neo4j write() + reasoner.update()), plus the explain grounding in bff/src/safety/explain.ts.
InvariantNo new write path. Every change is a reversible, PROV‑O‑logged change‑set through HITL. MCP stays read‑only.

F4 · cheapest win · mostly wiring

F5 · horizon (not in this build set)
Predictive / early‑warning tier
Beyond binary pass/fail — flag subjects trending toward a violation (a follow‑up due soon, a lab about to age out) as an amber “at‑risk” tier before they fail. SHACL warning shapes + a light propensity score. The reactive→predictive shift the field is betting on for 2026.
