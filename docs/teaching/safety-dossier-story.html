Reading the signal

02Disproportionality: PRR & ROR across the three immune-related events

Disproportionality asks a simple question: is this event reported more often for this drug than for all other drugs? Bars above the dashed threshold (PRR = 2) are candidate signals. Whiskers show the 95% confidence interval.

The key insight

03The biggest number is not the strongest signal

Hypothyroidism and colitis carry the highest disproportionality (PRR 7.0 and 6.3). Yet the dossier elevates myocarditis (PRR 4.2) as the priority signal — because it is the one with independent confirmation from multiple evidence types, not statistics alone. Disproportionality generates hypotheses; corroboration confirms them.

Disproportionality magnitude (PRR)

Independent corroborating evidence

Ranked by independent confirmation, myocarditis leads: FAERS + literature + FDA label + clinical registry + a mechanistic entailment.

Teaching point. This is exactly why the playbook routes to a human instead of auto-ranking by PRR. A high PRR can be inflated by reporting bias, notoriety, or a small denominator. The trustworthy signal is the one you can corroborate — and point at.
The math, briefly

04Where PRR and ROR come from — the 2×2 table

Both measures are built from one contingency table over the spontaneous-report database: the drug of interest vs. all other drugs, crossed with the event of interest vs. all other events.

Event of interestAll other events
This druga = 37b
All other drugscd

a is the case count the graph stores (37 for myocarditis). The graph keeps the computed statistics; the raw background cells (b, c, d) live in the source PV database.

PRR = [ a / (a+b) ] ÷ [ c / (c+d) ]   // event rate on the drug vs. everywhere else ROR = ( a / b ) ÷ ( c / d ) = (a·d) / (b·c)   // reporting odds ratio χ² = Yates-corrected test of independence CI = 95% interval; a signal needs lower bound > 1

Reading it: PRR 4.2 = myocarditis is reported ~4× more often for this drug than across the rest of the database. ROR runs slightly higher than PRR when the event is concentrated in the drug's reports. Full definitions, thresholds, and caveats live in DISPROPORTIONALITY.md.

Close the loop

07Route the conclusion to a human — and keep the receipt

The final checkpoint step enqueues the myocarditis conclusion to the HITL queue with its confidence (the faithfulness score) attached. A named reviewer (F6) approves it later; the approval is stamped into the audit trail. Nothing is auto-written to the graph.
